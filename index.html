How it works

Four steps to the front of the line

  1. 01

    Join in store

    Members only. Ask at the counter and we'll sign you up.

  2. 02

    Earn on every dollar

    $1 spent = 1 point. No points on allocated bottles, tobacco, beer or misc. items.

  3. 03

    Stack bonus points

    Every month, select bottles earn 2x, 5x or 10x points. See them below.

  4. 04

    Pick first on Bourbon Day

    Most points picks first from our allocated list. You redeem points to buy.

Once a year

Bourbon Day

At the end of the year, once enough allocated bottles come in, we hold Bourbon Day. Members pick from the list in points order: the top member chooses first, then the next, and so on.

Points are redeemed to buy bottles on Bourbon Day.

Questions

Good to know

How do I check my points?

Tap Check your rank and enter the phone number on your loyalty account. Questions about your total? Ask us at the counter.

Do my points expire?

No. Your account just needs a purchase at least every 60 days to stay active in the program.

What doesn't earn points?

Allocated bottles, tobacco, beer and misc. items.

What does "10x points" mean?

Bonus bottles earn more than the usual 1 point per $1. A 10x bottle earns 10 points for every dollar.

When is Bourbon Day?

At the end of the year, once enough allocated bottles have come in. Follow @taphouseliquor for updates.

*Points never expire while your account stays active. Rules and bottles are subject to change.
